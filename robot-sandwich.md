# Robot Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

## Assumptions
- **Two** stacked slices of white bread on a plate sitting on the counter
- A **closed** jar of peanut butter sitting on the counter
- A **closed** jar of jelly sitting on the counter next to the peanut butter
- **Two** butter knives sitting on the counter next to the plate
- A robot that has a set of functioning eyes and functioning arms with functioning empty hands attached that is sitting at the counter ready to make a sandwich

## Steps
1. Grab the peanut butter jar with your left hand
2. With your right hand rotate the lid counterclockwise until the lid comes completely loose
3. Place the peanut butter jar back on the counter
4. Grab the lid off the top of the peanut butter jar with your right hand
5. Place the lid on the counter next to the opened peanut butter jar
6. Grab the jelly jar with your left hand
7. With your right hand rotate the lid counterclockwise until the lid comes completely loose
8. Place the jelly jar back on the counter
9. Grab the lid off the top of the jelly jar with your right hand
10. Place the lid on the counter next to the opened jelly jar
11. Grab the peanut butter jar with your right hand
12. Grab the knife by the handle end that is closest to the plate with your left hand
13. Dip the flat end of the knife that is currently in your hand into the peanut butter
14. Scoop up enough peanut butter with the flat end of the knife currently inserted into the peanut butter to coat the flat end of the knife completely
15. Place the peanut butter jar back on the counter
16. Spread the peanut butter that is currently covered on the knife across the top piece of bread until the peanut butter is evenly spread
17. Put the knife away on the counter
18. Grab the jelly jar with your right hand
19. Grab the remaining knife that you have not used yet with your left hand
20. Dip the flat end of the knife that is currently in your hand into the jelly jar
21. Scoop up enough jelly to with the flat end of the knife currently inserted into the jelly to cover the coat the flat end of the knife completely
22. Place the jelly jar back on the counter
23. With your right hand grab the slice of bread coated in peanut butter
24. Place the slice of bread that you are currenclty holding in your right hand on the counter next to the plate
25. Spread the jelly that is currently covered on the knife across the piece of bread that remains on the plate until the jelly is evenly spread
26. Put the knife currently in your hand away next to the dirty peanut butter knife
27. Grab the bread on the counter that has the peanut butter on it with your right hand
28. Place the side that has the peanut butter on it on top of the jelly bread that is on the plate so that the peanut butter and jelly are touching each other

## Done When
- Two slices of bread are on top of each other with peanut butter and jelly between them
- Both knives are on the counter and the robots hands are empty
